As nuvens sobre Sapucaia

Isaac Clayton (2026) · pixels, 14 colors, Aseprite, 192x256 · Cambridge, Massachusetts

Statement

I made this pixel art while listening to lecture. It’s based on a photo I took of some clouds over a small neighborhood on a hill in Sapucaia, RS, Brazil. I like how the clouds turned out. I used colors from the Eroge Copper palette, but had to add an additional shade of green. Can you find the hidden heart?
